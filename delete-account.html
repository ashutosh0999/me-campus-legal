Delete your account

Last updated: 3 October 2026

⚖️ NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW. This page describes what the app actually does. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before relying on it, have someone qualified check the grace period and the retention rules against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

This page explains how to delete your me_campus account and the data attached to it. It applies to the me_campus app (com.mecampus.app).

me_campus never takes payments, so there are no card, bank or billing records to delete — we have never held any.

1. Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest route, and it is the one to use if you can still sign in.

1. Open me_campus and sign in. 2. Go to Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account. 3. Confirm.

Your account stops working immediately.

2. Request deletion without the app

Use this if you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or have lost access to your college email.

Email ashutoshrajput0909@gmail.com from any address, with the subject "Delete my account", and include:

  • - the college email address you signed up with (for example

24bcsXXXXX@cuchd.in), and

  • - your name as it appears in the app, if you remember it.

We may ask one question to confirm the account is yours before erasing it — otherwise anyone could delete anyone else's account by knowing their email address.

We reply to every request. If you have not heard back within seven days, send the email again; it is a small project and mail does occasionally go astray.

3. What happens, and when

WhenWhat
ImmediatelyThe account stops working. You cannot browse PGs, appear in collaboration search, or send or receive requests.
For 14 daysYou can cancel and get the account back, by signing in and choosing Cancel deletion. This exists so a mistake, or someone else using your phone, is not permanent.
Within 30 days of your requestThe erasure below is completed.

4. What is deleted

  • - Your profile: name, course, year, photo, interests, gender if you set it
  • - Your collaboration profile, including your skills and your links
  • - PGs you saved
  • - Notifications sent to you
  • - The device records used to send you push notifications
  • - Your sign-in credential

5. What is kept, with your name removed

  • - Collaboration requests you sent or received. The other student took part

in that exchange and keeps their own record of it. Erasing it would rewrite their history, not only yours, so your identity is replaced with a placeholder and the exchange itself remains.

  • - Enquiries you sent to PG owners, for 12 months, with your identity

replaced. Owners need a record of who contacted them about a room.

6. What is kept in full

  • - Reports filed about you, and reports you filed. If deleting an account

erased the complaints against it, deleting an account would be how you escape a complaint.

  • - Moderation and admin records. These are append-only by design: a log

that can be edited is not a log.

These records are kept because deleting them would harm other people's safety or their own records, not for marketing, analytics or any commercial purpose. They are never used to contact you after your account is gone.
