me_campus

Find a PG near campus, and students to build things with.

me_campus is a campus app for students at Chandigarh University. It is a discovery and connection service: it never collects rent, deposits, brokerage or any payment, and holds no card or bank details.

This site hosts the documents the app links to:

  • Privacy Policy — what is collected, who can see it, and how to delete it.
  • Terms of Use — what the app is, and what a verification badge does and does not mean.
  • Delete your account — how to erase your account, from inside the app or by email if you have uninstalled it.

The first two are also reachable from inside the app, under Profile → Privacy & data.

me_campus is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Chandigarh University or any other institution.
