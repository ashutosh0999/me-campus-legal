What we never collect

  • - Your phone number — if you are a student. We never ask for it. When you

contact a PG owner you see their number and message them yourself. (PG owners are different: they sign in with a phone number, which is how they are contactable at all. See §4a.)

  • - Payment or bank details. We handle no money at all.
  • - Your location. Distances are calculated from your campus, not from you.
  • - The content of your messages. When you tap "WhatsApp" or "Call" we record

that contact happened, never what was said. Those conversations happen in your own apps and we cannot see them.

  • - Contacts, photos library, microphone, or your other apps.

4a. If you are a PG owner

PG owners do not use this app — they use a separate web portal. What we hold about an owner is different, and worth stating plainly because the same service holds both:

DataWhy
Phone numberIt is how an owner signs in, and the number a student calls about a room. Verified by a one-time code.
Business or owner name, description, photoShown on a listing so a student knows who they are dealing with
Listings: address, rent, rooms, facilities, photosThe listing itself

An owner's phone number is not stored on the listing and never appears in a search result. A student gets it only when they choose to contact that owner, through a server-side check that limits how many owners one student can pull numbers for in a day. We record that the contact happened, never what was said.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account from Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account, inside the app. If you have already uninstalled it, or cannot sign in, see Delete your account — it explains how to request deletion by email, and lists in full what is erased and what is kept.

Your account stops working immediately. After a 14-day grace period, during which you can change your mind, it is deleted.

Deleted: your profile, photo, interests, collaboration profile, saved PGs, notifications sent to you, and your sign-in.

Kept, with your name removed:

  • - Collaboration requests you sent or received. The other student took part

in that exchange and keeps their own record of it; erasing it would rewrite their history, not just yours.

  • - Enquiries you sent to PG owners, for 12 months, because owners need a

record of who contacted them.

Kept in full:

  • - Reports filed about you, and reports you filed. If deleting an account

erased the reports against it, deleting an account would be how you escape a complaint.

  • - Moderation and admin records, which are append-only by design.

⚖️ NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW — whether retaining anonymised moderation records satisfies the DPDP Act's erasure obligations, and whether 14 days is the right grace period.

7. Children

me_campus is for enrolled college students. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

  • - Access is enforced by server-side rules, not by the app. The app cannot grant

itself permissions it does not have.

  • - Your college identity is verified from a signed token issued by Google, which

a client cannot forge.

  • - Provider phone numbers are stored separately from listings so they cannot

leak into a search result.

  • - No one can read another student's private data, and PG owners cannot read

student records at all.

We cannot promise perfect security, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. We can tell you what we actually do, which is the above.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating this page.
