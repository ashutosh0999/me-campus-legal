1. What me_campus is

A way to find a PG near your campus, and to find other students to build things with. That is all it is.

What it is not:

  • - a broker, agent or landlord,
  • - a party to any agreement you make with a PG owner,
  • - a payment service.

2. We never handle money

me_campus does not collect rent, deposits, brokerage, booking fees or any payment. We hold no card or bank details.

Anyone asking you to pay me_campus is not us. Pay the owner directly, in person, after you have seen the place.

3. Who can use it

You need a verified email address from the pilot college, and you must be 18 or over. One account per person. Do not share your account.

4. What verification means, exactly

When a listing shows a verification badge, it names the specific checks that were performed — for example "4 of 5 checks". It means those checks, by a person, on the date shown. Nothing more.

It is not a guarantee about the property, the owner, the safety of the building, or the accuracy of anything the owner told us. We check what we can check and we say which checks those were.

Always visit in person before you commit to anything or hand over money.

5. Your responsibilities

Do not:

  • - pretend to be someone else, or make a profile for someone else,
  • - harass, threaten or abuse anyone,
  • - post anything false, obscene, discriminatory or illegal,
  • - use the app to advertise, recruit or sell,
  • - scrape, bulk-collect or resell anything from the app, including owner

contact details,

  • - try to get around the security rules or the anti-spam limits.

You can report and block anyone, at any time, from their profile.

6. PG owners and listings

Listings are submitted by owners. Owners are responsible for their accuracy.

An owner must confirm a listing is still current periodically. When they stop, students are told the listing is unchecked, and eventually it stops being shown. We would rather show you an honest "last checked 40 days ago" than a confident lie.

We can refuse, suspend or remove any listing, and we can suspend any account. When we do, the reason is recorded.

7. What we do not promise

The app is provided as it is. We do not promise it will be available at all times, or that every listing is accurate, or that you will find a room or a collaborator.

We are a student project running on a free tier, and we would rather say that plainly than imply a service level we cannot deliver.

8. Disputes between you and someone else

Anything you agree with a PG owner is between you and that owner. Anything that happens on a collaboration is between you and the other student. We are not a party to it and we cannot enforce it.

If something goes wrong, report it in the app. We can act on accounts and listings. We cannot recover your money, and we will not pretend otherwise.
